Garber's Heart for Service

Giving back has always been at the heart of our business.

Guy Garber served 30 years as president of the Saginaw Society for Crippled Children, helped organize the Community Chest, built a "Kids Roost" grandstand so children could watch the Saginaw Aces for free, and gave the city its tennis courts. Three generations later, giving back is still how Garber shows up in every town it serves.

Our Impact

What a family business owes the town that built it.

Garber gives back more than $1.5 million to community organizations every year and have led capital campaigns for the YMCA, the CAN Council and the Temple Theatre. Garber team members add thousands of volunteer hours on top — because giving is part of the culture, not a line in the budget.

Our Partners

A group that acts like a hometown dealer.

Eight national nonprofits have a standing place at every Garber store. We sponsor their events, our teams show up to work them — Light The Night walks, Habitat builds, Heart Walks, Relay For Life, Red Cross blood drives — and the company gives alongside. Junior Achievement gets Garber people in classrooms; Special Olympics and the Boys & Girls Clubs get our support for the athletes and kids who count on them.

That's the group commitment. On top of it, each store backs the causes its own team and customers care about — and if you know one that needs a hand, we'd like to hear about it.
