Our History

A century of automotive pioneering.

In 1907, most of Michigan still thought the horse and buggy was here to stay. Guy S. Garber, a 23-year-old farm-implement salesman, did not. Billy Durant — the founder of General Motors — noticed, and put him on the road selling Buicks and opening dealerships across Michigan.

He came to Saginaw for Buick in 1910, bought out the factory's retail interest in 1912, and by 1913 held the wholesale territory from Saginaw County to the Straits of Mackinac — one of the original Buick Thirteen. His work with GM lasted nearly 60 years, and when he died in 1965 his was the last Buick distributorship in America.

Where the name first went over a door: R. J. Garber Farm Implements, 414 N. Washington Avenue, Lansing.

Guy Garber at work in the Saginaw office.

1907

Guy S. Garber goes to work for Buick

At William C. Durant's urging, the 23-year-old salesman becomes a Buick representative and opens a dealership in Battle Creek.

1912

Garber Buick is born

Buick leaves the retail business; Guy buys its Saginaw Valley interest. A year later he is sole owner and one of the Buick Thirteen distributors.

1965

The last distributorship

Guy Garber dies at 81. Richard Garber Sr. takes over as president and owner.

1980

Richard J. Garber Jr.

The third generation becomes president in the company's 73rd year — the youngest Buick dealer in the nation.

1986

Community

Giving back has always been at the heart of our business.

Guy Garber founded the Saginaw Society for Crippled Children, helped organize the local division of the United Way, built the Garber Tennis Courts and served on the Saginaw City School Board. Three generations on, Richard J. Garber Jr. has led capital campaigns for the YMCA, the CAN Council and the Temple Theatre — and Garber team members put in thousands of volunteer hours every year.

At Garber Buick, that looks like the Great Lakes Bay Region organizations we sponsor, the events our team shows up for, and the causes our customers bring to us. If it matters to Saginaw, it matters here.
